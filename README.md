# 🏥 My Favorite Series: *Doctor X: Age of the White Mafia*

## 📌 Overview

**Doctor X: Age of the White Mafia** (닥터X: 하얀 마피아의 시대) is a *medical noir dark comedy* K-drama on **SBS** and **Netflix**. It is the ***Korean adaptation*** of the Japanese drama *Doctor-X: Surgeon Michiko Daimon*.

> In a hospital run by corruption and bureaucracy, a genius surgeon known as the "mad dog of the operating room" earns respect through her skills alone.

## 👥 Main Characters

- **Kim Ji-won** as Gye Su-jeong / *Doctor X*
  - A gifted freelance surgeon sent to GUMC
- **Lee Jung-eun** as Jang Hui-suk
  - Director of a doctor agency and Su-jeong's aunt by marriage
- **Son Hyun-joo** as Bu Seung-kwon
  - Director of the GUMC hospital
- **Kim Woo-seok** as Park Tae-gyeong
  - A warm-hearted surgical intern
- **Lee Jin-hee** as Jo Won-jin
  - A cynical but skilled anesthesiology professor

---

## ⭐ Why I Like It

1. **Kim Ji-won** is amazing as a confident, fearless surgeon.
2. It mixes *medical drama*, *mystery*, and *comedy* really well.
3. It shows how Su-jeong fights against a corrupt hospital system.
4. The surgery scenes are **intense and exciting**.
